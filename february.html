niagara falls! ♡

✦ ♡ ✦

journey behind the falls

address: 6650 niagara parkway
visit website ↗
we go down into tunnels behind the horseshoe falls. it's year-round, but the lower observation deck closes during winter because of ice :c

niagara parks power station + tunnel

address: 7005 niagara parkway
visit website ↗
old hydroelectric power station, exhibits and a MASSIVE underground tunnel :3 this is one of the less obvious tourist thingy

floral showhouse

address: 7145 niagara parkway
visit website ↗
another warm tropical escape from february, with orchids too :p

niagara skywheel

address: 4960 clifton hill
visit website ↗
heated and cooled enclosed gondolas and views of the falls, and it's open year round :D

clifton hill

address: 4960 clifton hill
visit website ↗
arcades, rides, mini golf, skywheel and a bunch of other random attractions c:

dufferin islands

address: 6345 dufferin isle road
visit website ↗
short paths, little islands, bridges and wildlife. it's much more of a gentle walk than a hike

niagara glen nature centre

address: 3050 niagara parkway
visit website ↗
this is the nature option i'd look at if you become okay with a short winter walk

queenston heights

address: 14184 niagara parkway, niagara-on-the-lake
visit website ↗
viewpoints, history, trails and brock's monument without needing to spend the entire day hiking

niagara-on-the-lake

address: 122 queen street, niagara-on-the-lake
visit website ↗
cute historic town, little shops, cafes, antique stores and pretty streets. this would be really nice if we want a slower day

niagara falls butterfly conservatory night?

okay this isn't actually a separate attraction but if they have any special evening event when we're there, I REALLY WANNA DO IT

niagara whirlpool

location: niagara parkway
visit website ↗
this is one of those places that's easy to overlook because it's not the falls, but the water doing the giant whirlpool thing is actually really cool xd

niagara falls illumination

location: niagara falls
visit website ↗
the falls get lit up at night. obviously we've gotta see the falls during the day but seeing them lit up at night would be really pretty too<3
