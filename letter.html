happy 5 months my love :3

five months... i don't know how we've already gotten here. it feels like just yesterday i was some random high idiot ragdolled inside of a chimney, and then somehow that little moment ended up becoming one of the most important things that's ever happened to me >~<

five months with you feels both so long and so short at the same time. there's been so many memories, so many late nights, so many conversations, so many times we've laughed until we couldn't anymore, and so many moments where i've just sat there and thought about how lucky i am that i met you.

i've been thinking a lot about us lately, especially because it's been five months now, and i wanted to actually sit down and put everything i feel into words. i know i can be bad at explaining how much you mean to me sometimes, and sometimes i get stuck in my own head and don't know how to say things properly, but i really want you to know just how much i love you. so i decided to make this as long as i possibly could because.. you deserve that much and so much more.

♡ ✦ ♡ ✦ ♡

i want to start with how we even met, because it's still so fucking funny to me when i think about it xd

we met one night in meet people across the world. i was high as fuck, i was incredibly shy, and for some reason i was in a chimney.. i don't even know how i ended up there xd, and then you walked over to me >o<

you were wearing the cat outfit, and i remember seeing you walk over and say hello. i was so quiet because i was high and shy as hell, and honestly i'm still pretty shy sometimes xd. i probably didn't give you much of an impression at first, but that night ended up being one of the best nights of my life simply because it was the night i met you.

it's so crazy to think about how something so random could lead to all of this. out of every person you could've walked over to that night, you walked over to me. out of every stupid roblox game we could've been playing, we happened to be in that one. and out of every possible night, it happened that night.

and now five months later, i'm sitting here writing an entire fucking essay about you ^o^

i wouldn't change that night for anything..

🌸 ♡ 🌸 ♡ 🌸

i think the first time i really realized i liked you was about a week after we met. we stayed up playing games and talking for what felt like forever. we started getting into deeper conversations, talking about things that weren't just random conversations anymore, and we watched the office together and laughed our asses off.

somehow we ended up staying awake until morning.

i remember just feeling so comfortable with you. i didn't want the night to end. i didn't want to stop talking to you. i remember thinking about how easy it felt to just exist around you, even though we'd only known each other for such a short amount of time.

and that's when i started realizing that you weren't just some person i met online.

i actually liked you.

i liked talking to you. i liked hearing what you had to say. i liked spending time with you. i liked how easily you could make me laugh. and more than anything, i liked how i felt when i was with you.

💗 ✦ 💗 ✦ 💗

then eventually came the first time we said i love you.

it was shortly after you asked me to be your boyfriend, and i told you i loved you. honestly, i don't think you could've asked me in a better way. i don't even know how to explain how happy i was in that moment. there was something about finally being able to call you my girlfriend that just felt right to me..

and now i get to look back at that moment and realize that the girl i was so excited to call my girlfriend five months ago is still the person i want beside me now.

🎀 ♡ 🎀 ♡ 🎀

one of my favourite memories is actually one of those conversations we had when we were still just talking.

we were both laying down, it was really late at night, and somehow the conversation slowly turned into us getting really close and telling each other things that were much more personal. we talked about trauma, things we'd been through, things that weren't necessarily easy to talk about.

i remember how vulnerable that felt.

and i think that was one of the moments where i realized how much i trusted you.

because being able to tell someone those things isn't easy. letting someone see the parts of you that you normally hide is scary. but with you, i wanted to. i wanted you to know me. not just the funny parts, not just the parts of myself i'm comfortable showing people, but the messy parts too.

and you've always made me feel like those parts of me don't make me less worthy of being loved.

🐱 💕 🐱 💕 🐱

there are also so many little things you do that i don't think you even realize how adorable they are.

one of my favourites is that little noise you make when you're thinking about something. i know it's a chilean thing and a lot of people there do it, but i don't care because when YOU do it, it's the cutest thing ever..

i don't even know why it makes me so happy c:

it's just one of those tiny little things that makes me think "that's my girlfriend!"

there are so many things like that too. little mannerisms, little reactions, the way you talk, the way you get excited about things, the way you react when you're interested in something. i don't think you realize how much i notice those little things.

and i love them.

i love the little things just as much as the big things.

🌷 ✨ 🌷 ✨ 🌷

when it comes to games, one of my favourite things we've done technically wasn't even us playing together.

i got you to install firewatch, one of my favorite indie games, and i watched you play it.

and i loved that.

there was something so nice about watching you experience something that i already loved.. getting to see your reactions, watching you explore everything, and just knowing that something i loved was becoming something you could enjoy too.

but if i had to choose a game we actually played together, i'd probably say stardew valley.

there's just something so relaxing about farming, fishing, mining, and doing all the little pointless things together.

and minecraft too :3

i love those kinds of games with you because we don't really have to do anything special. we can just exist together. we can build something stupid, wander around, get distracted by something completely unrelated, and somehow that's enough to make me happy.

i think that's one of the things i've come to love most about being with you.

i don't need some huge exciting event to feel happy with you. sometimes i just want to sit there and play a stupid game with you while we talk.

sometimes i just want to stay up way too late.

sometimes i want to watch something together.

sometimes i just want to hear your voice.

and somehow all of those little things mean so much to me.

💕 🐱 💕 🐱 💕

you also make me feel safe.

and honestly, this one is difficult to explain because it's not really one specific moment where you made me feel safe.

you ALWAYS make me feel safe.

whenever i'm struggling with something, you immediately let me know that you're there for me. you listen to me, you reassure me, you stay on my side, and you make me feel like i don't have to deal with everything alone.

there have been so many times where i've been struggling with something and you've just been there.

and i don't think i'll ever be able to properly explain how much that means to me.

feeling safe with someone is something i don't take for granted.

♡ ✦ ♡ ✦ ♡

especially because when i first started talking to you, there were things about myself that i was genuinely scared you would judge me for.

one of the biggest things was my education and how behind i felt in life.

i was scared to tell you because i didn't know how you'd see me after you found out. i was scared you'd think i was less intelligent, less capable, or somehow less of a person because i hadn't gone through life the same way other people had.

but when i finally told you, you were understanding.

you never made me feel like i was less.

i still remember you telling me that everyone goes at their own pace.

and that genuinely stuck with me.

i think about that sometimes when i'm feeling like i'm behind everyone else.

because you reminded me that my life isn't a race.

i don't have to have everything figured out immediately.

and having someone i love tell me that and actually mean it made me feel so much better.

🌸 💗 🌸 💗 🌸

there are also things i've learned about myself through these five months.

one of the biggest things i've realized is that i have a lot of problems with attachment.

i've realized how easily i can get scared of losing someone i love, how easily my brain can start overthinking things, and how sometimes my own insecurities can make things harder than they need to be.

and i know those are things i'm going to have to keep working on.

not because you demanded that i change who i am, but because i want to be better.

for myself, but also for you.

i want to be someone who can love you without constantly being controlled by fear.

i want to be someone who can trust the person he loves (not saying i don’t)

i want to be someone who can give you the same sense of safety that you give me.

and i know i'm not going to magically become perfect overnight.

i'm still going to mess up.

i'm still going to have moments where i overthink.

i'm still going to have things i need to work through.

but i promise i'm going to keep trying.

because you are worth the effort.

our relationship is worth the effort.

and i want to keep growing alongside you instead of letting my fears control me.
